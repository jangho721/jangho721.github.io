01. Course recommendation

One of my primary focus areas was course recommendation for Samsung Multicampus, helping learners find courses that fit their learning history.

  • Raised enrollment conversion by 5% and completion by 25% over the previous recommender by developing a course recommender that uses learning histories and BERT-based title similarity.

02. Learner diagnosis & problem recommendation

I also worked on diagnosing what learners know and recommending problems that match, helping digital mathematics textbooks adapt to each student.

  • Improved knowledge-tracing models for learner diagnosis by engineering new features from WAPL Math, an AI-based mathematics learning platform.
  • Led development of a problem recommender tailored to student proficiency for digital mathematics textbooks, gathering requirements in client meetings.

03. MLOps

I built the pipelines that keep recommendations current and deliver them to clients.

  • Built Airflow pipelines for retraining and batch inference, writing per-user recommendations to the databases of about 800 client sites.
